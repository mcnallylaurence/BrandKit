# Logos

The mark is a line-drawn shamrock in a circle. The wordmark is "Ladybird Ever After" in Rolling Pen Basic Two.

Use the files in this folder. Drop extra versions, SVGs, or print files in [`drop-here`](drop-here).

## Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark at least equal to the height of one shamrock leaf. Do not crowd it with type, badges, or other logos.

## Minimum size

- Shamrock: 24px on screen, 10mm in print
- Long wordmark: 120px wide on screen
- Stacked wordmark: 72px wide on screen

If it gets smaller than that, use the shamrock alone.

## Do

- Use the green mark on cream.
- Use the white mark on photos and green bands.
- Keep the shamrock a circle. Do not crop it into a square.
- Keep the wordmark intact. Do not retype it in another script.

## Don't

- Do not recolor the mark to rose, brass, or gold.
- Do not add a drop shadow, outline, or glow.
- Do not stretch, rotate, or put the mark in a hard box.
- Do not place the green mark on a mid-tone photo. Switch to white, or use the cream-disc version.
- Do not pair the wordmark with a second script font.
- Do not lock the shamrock up next to a partner logo at the same size. Ladybird stays larger.
