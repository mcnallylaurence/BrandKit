# Do and don't

## Color

Do lead with green on cream, with charcoal type and one rose moment.

Don't use the retired gold `#B8964E`, dusty blue, lavender, or more than one accent.

## Type

Do use Bree Serif for headings and Nunito for body, buttons, and nav.

Don't load Playfair Display, Alice, or Recoleta Alt.

## Logos

Do use the green shamrock on cream and the white wordmark on photos.

Don't recolor, stretch, or box the mark. Don't retype the wordmark in another script.

## Shape

Do round photos and cards to 16px. Do round buttons to 12px.

Don't mix sharp photos with rounded cards on the same page. Don't make buttons into full pills.

## Copy

Do write like Yasmeen or Laurence would say it out loud.

Don't use em dashes, fairytale language, Irish slang, or corporate filler.

## Photos

Do use real Ladybird couples and real Irish venues.

Don't use generic stock, heavy filters, or posed catalogue couples.
