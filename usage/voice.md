# Voice

Ladybird sounds like a trusted friend who has actually done this. Warm, expert, and calm with American couples planning an Irish wedding from home.

Yasmeen and Laurence planned their own wedding in Ireland from the US. That is the voice. A real person sharing real knowledge. Not a corporation.

## How we sound

- Clear and practical. Say what it costs, how long it takes, and what happens next.
- Warm. Couples are planning from 4,000 miles away. Make them feel safe.
- Slightly playful, never cheesy.
- Honest about the difference between marrying in Ireland and marrying at home.
- Inclusive. Do not assume gender, guest count, or a first wedding.

## Phrases that belong

- "We handle the stress. You get the joy."
- "One USD contract. One point of contact."
- "You never send money to Ireland."
- "Here's what you need to know."
- "We've guided 100+ couples through this."

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| Magical, fairytale, enchanted | Beautiful, meaningful, real |
| Curate, bespoke, artisan | Choose, custom, trusted |
| Leverage, synergize | Use, bring together |
| Craic, top of the morning | Plain English about Irish traditions |
| Click here | The name of the page |

## Punctuation

No em dashes or en dashes. Use a hyphen or rewrite.

## Product language

Two public lines:

- **Ladybird Packages:** Fáilte, Grá, Sláinte, Ard. Most couples. Wedding budgets from $5,900 to $50,000.
- **Ladybird Luxury:** Full wedding planning for wedding budgets of $50,000+.

Irish package names with fadas stay on Packages and Ways to Plan. Ads and the homepage use English size language: the two of you, a small circle, an intimate wedding, a full wedding day.

Do not mention Ladybird Lite in public work.
