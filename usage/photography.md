# Photography

Real weddings. Real venues. Real Ireland.

## What to use

- Ladybird couple photography, used with permission
- Natural light, landscape, stone, weather, and quiet moments
- Faces when we have consent. Details when we don't

## What to avoid

- Generic stock
- Over-saturated "Emerald Isle" filters
- Heavy vignettes or fake film grain
- Posed catalogue couples who are not ours

## Treatment on the web

- Photos, cards, and frames: `border-radius: 16px` and `overflow: hidden`
- Full-bleed heroes stay square to the screen edge so they do not float
- A soft dark overlay is fine on heroes so cream type stays readable
- Prefer a slight desaturate at rest, then full color on hover, if the page already does that

## Credits

Name the photographer when the layout has room. The site footer already lists the regulars. Keep that habit on print and decks.

## Alt text

Describe what is actually in the picture, plus the place.

Good: "Bride and groom at a Cliffs of Moher elopement ceremony"

Not good: "wedding photo"
